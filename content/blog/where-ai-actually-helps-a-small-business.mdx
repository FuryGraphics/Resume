Most small business owners have been sold AI as a website chat widget that nobody uses. The useful version is much less flashy, and it starts with the phone.

## The call you didn't answer

You are on a job, with a customer, or asleep. The phone rings and you can't pick up. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list, and that business gets the work.

The research on this is old and still holds: the business that responds first usually wins. Speed matters more than polish.

## What the text back does

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text from your number within seconds. The assistant answers the common questions from information you have approved, collects the details of the job, and books the appointment. Every conversation is passed to you, and you can take over at any point.

## What it should not do

- Make promises you would not make yourself
- Guess at prices or availability it has not been given
- Pretend to be you when the customer asks

Good AI for a small business is narrow. It does one job well and hands everything else to a person.

## Where to start

If you are going to try one piece of AI in your business, make it this one. It works on the leads you already have, before you spend anything on getting more.
