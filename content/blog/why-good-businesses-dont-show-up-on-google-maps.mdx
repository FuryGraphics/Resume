When someone nearby searches for what you do, Google shows a map with three businesses under it. Those three get most of the calls. Everyone else is a scroll away, and most people never scroll.

The businesses in that top three are not always the best at the job. They are the ones Google understands best.

## The profile is half empty

Your Google Business Profile is the first thing Google reads about you. Most profiles have the name, the phone number, and not much else.

- **Categories** that match what customers actually search for, not just the closest generic one
- **Services** listed one by one, in the words customers use
- **Photos** of real work, added over time, not once at setup
- **Questions and answers** filled in before anyone has to ask

## Your details don't match

Your business name, address, and phone number live on dozens of sites you never think about: map apps, directories, voice assistants, in-car GPS. When they disagree, Google is less sure who you are and where you work. Keeping them identical everywhere is dull work, and it matters.

## Nothing is happening

A profile that has not changed in a year looks like a business that might not be open. Regular posts, fresh photos, and a steady stream of new reviews tell Google you are active.
